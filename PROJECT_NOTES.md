  - Results: score, percentage, per-domain results, and a review of flagged, missed, and unanswered questions. Score colors are labeled as personal goals.
  - Saved progress: browser storage is required, keyed to stable question IDs and revisions, with a confirmed reset.
  - Practice Exam: resumes after a refresh, keeping the original deadline. At least 10 questions per domain; the rest of the blueprint is set after the pilot.
  - Release: 100 distinct approved questions meeting the blueprint, plus content, scoring, storage, accessibility, and usability checks. A Learning Mode beta is undecided.
