# Research: Certified Archivist Examination

Prepared by Plexy (Perplexity) for Phase 1. All facts checked October 11, 2026.

Sources are official Academy of Certified Archivists (ACA) pages unless noted. "Source date" is the date the source itself gives (publication, approval, or update); "Not shown" means the page carries no date. Re-verify before launch (Phase 5).

## Summary

- The exam is 100 multiple-choice questions in three hours, one correct answer each, no penalty for guessing, and questions can be flagged and revisited.
- It is based on the Role Delineation Statement (RDS), revised 2023–2024 and approved in 2025: 8 domains, plus a General Knowledge section. All RDS items total 85 knowledge statements and 119 tasks.
- There are no education or work prerequisites, effective with the 2026 exam.
- The next exam is June 10–12, 2027, online only, with applications open March 1 – April 30, 2027.
- Questions "come directly from the literature" on the annual Reading List. The current list is the 2026 Reading List, with 56 sources, each tagged by domain. A 2027 list was not posted as of the check date.
- ACA does not publish a numeric passing score or domain weights. Results are reported as High Pass, Pass, Low Pass, or Did Not Pass, with domain-level feedback.

## Role Delineation Statement

Current version: revised 2023–2024 by a task force chaired by Gerrianne Schaad, approved by the ACA Board in 2025, and announced with the 2025 Reading List ([RDS page](https://www.certifiedarchivists.org/25-Role-Delineation-Statement); [ACA announcement](https://www.certifiedarchivists.org/node/907)). ACA reviews the RDS at least every five years. The previous version (2019–2020, approved 2021) had seven domains; [its PDF](https://www.certifiedarchivists.org/sites/default/files/2022-04/RDS_2019-2020_Final.pdf) is still online, so watch for outdated domain lists.

## Reading List

Current version: [2026 Reading List](https://www.certifiedarchivists.org/26-Reading-List), compiled by the Examination Development Committee for the 2026 exam ([PDF](https://certifiedarchivists.org/sites/default/files/2025-01/ReadingList_ForOnline2.pdf)). Checked Oct 11, 2026; no 2027 list was posted yet.

Key points from ACA:
- Questions "come directly from the literature," but not one-to-one: a source may yield several questions or none in a given year.
- The list is reviewed annually, adding recent publications and removing outdated ones.
- The exam covers archival practice in the United States.
- Candidates are not expected to read everything.

## Couldn't verify

- **Domain weighting** (how many questions per domain): not published on any ACA page reviewed. ACA says only that each exam "represents all eight domains."
- **Passing score**: ACA reports result categories, not a numeric cut score. Third-party claims of "70 scaled" or "75%" are unsupported by ACA.

## Conflicts and outdated information

- **Deadline for first-year dues after passing.** [Examination Information](https://www.certifiedarchivists.org/examination-information) says within 30 days of receiving results. [Understanding Your CA Credential](https://www.certifiedarchivists.org/my-credential) says by September 30 following notice of passing, and states that the governing policy controls. Treat September 30 as current, and confirm with ACA if the app mentions it.
- **Third-party sites are out of date.** For example, [USPQ's guide](https://www.uspq.pro/certification/ca-arch-guide) lists degree and experience prerequisites and Pearson VUE test centers, and [Credential Guide](https://credentialguide.com/credentials/archivalconservation-certification) lists a $250 fee and a 75% pass mark. ACA's pages contradict these. Do not use either site as a source.
- **Seven vs. eight domains.** Older ACA and third-party documents list seven domains. Cultural Competency (D8) is part of the current RDS.

## Implications for the app

These are suggestions for the spec, not decisions.

- A full practice exam mode could mirror the real one: 100 questions, a three-hour timer, flag-and-review, and one correct answer per question.
- Tag each question with an RDS ID (domain, plus task or knowledge statement where possible) and a Reading List source. This mirrors how ACA maps its own item bank.
- Avoid showing a "pass" percentage, since ACA publishes none. Domain-level results match what ACA reports.
- Because of the NDA, never use recalled questions from past test takers. Write original questions from the RDS and Reading List.
- Since the RDS was published by ACA, link to it rather than copying the full text into this public repo.
